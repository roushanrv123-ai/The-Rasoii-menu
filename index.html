About The Rasoii

The Rasoii is a family restaurant in New Market, Katihar. We serve a multi-cuisine menu: North Indian curries, tandoor kebabs, Indo-Chinese favourites and Kolkata-style dum biryani, with desserts, shakes and mocktails to finish.

Every dish is cooked fresh to your spice level. Tell our team how spicy you like it, and we will cook it that way.
